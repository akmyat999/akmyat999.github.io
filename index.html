Selected projects

Dublin Liveability Index

  • Python
  • GeoPandas
  • OpenStreetMap
  • CSO Small Areas

A four-pillar Composite Liveability Index for 5,076 Dublin Small Areas, combining public transit access (400 m), active travel network density, healthcare access (500 m) and green space. 284 areas classed as Very Low liveability, and 62 areas identified with combined transport and healthcare access gaps.

UCD Transit Accessibility

  • ArcGIS Network Analyst
  • Service areas
  • Closest facility

Compared a 5-minute circular buffer with a network-based walking service area to the main UCD bus stop, and used closest-facility routing for six sample student locations. The buffer overstated access, and four of six locations were within a 5-minute walk of their nearest stop.

Dublin LiDAR Flood Susceptibility Modelling

  • ArcGIS Pro
  • 2 m LiDAR
  • AHP
  • OPW CFRAM validation

An AHP-based multi-criteria model on a 2 m LiDAR terrain model (28.87 million cells). It captured 98.08% of OPW historic flood outlines and 99.32% of the CFRAM 10-year flood extent.

Myanmar Township-Level Conflict Analysis

  • PySAL
  • Getis-Ord Gi*
  • Local Moran's I
  • KNN weights (K=8)

Analysed ACLED fatalities across 330 townships (Moran's I = 0.428). Identified 22 Gi* hotspots and 6 High-High clusters, and found that the top 10% of townships account for about 85% of recorded fatalities.

Dublin Land-Cover Mapping: Deep Learning vs. OBIA-SVM

  • ArcGIS Pro Image Analyst
  • U-Net
  • Sentinel-2

Compared a ResNet-34 U-Net (about 98% validation accuracy) with an object-based SVM classifier on Sentinel-2 imagery, and examined river continuity and shadow-related errors.
